A road has to kill before anyone flags it.

An Assam road stretch becomes an official blackspot only after 5 serious crashes or 10 deaths. Driver-assist cars and road engineers get no warning before that. In 48 hours we set out to collect the evidence that could give one.

Our question

Which 500 m of road is dangerous, and what evidence would show it before the crashes pile up?

Users: driver-assist systems that read the road ahead, and the engineers who decide what to fix first.

What OSM does not know

Lanes are tagged on 1% of roads, speed limits on 0.4%, and only 28 speed breakers are mapped in all of Assam. We relied on geometry instead of tags.

Step 5 · feature engineering: what the data actually carries

52 candidate features, tested source by source, 33 kept.

How we narrowed them (judged only on Jul 2024 – Jun 2025)
Information lost when one source is removed

Road class carries the most; geometry and nearby crash history next; pedestrian places add almost nothing once road density and light are known. PCA needed 19 components for 90% of the variance, so the sources are not redundant, and compressing them made results worse.

Problem we caught in our own work

When we rebuilt our earlier model with the new feature code, it scored 43.3% instead of 48.7%. Floating-point scraps were leaking each piece's own crash count into its "nearby crashes" feature. We switched to an exact search, got an exact match, and reran everything. The leak would have faked a 6-point gain.

Problem in the data

Uneven growth in police reporting made old crash history partly a map of where iRAD was adopted. We added drift-corrected history features (nearby crashes ÷ the region's own reporting rate).

Rain looks "safer" because fewer people travel. Without traffic counts we can't separate that, which is exactly why the live collection matters.

Models used, deliberately simple

Negative-binomial crash regression + boosted Poisson trees, blended with each road piece's own record (Empirical Bayes); case-crossover conditional logit for weather; logistic regression for severity. Trains in about 3 minutes on a laptop.

What changes, and what comes next

The evidence exists. It just had to be collected.

An engineer can already rank 4,060 km of road to inspect first. A driver-assist system could read the same ranking as a risk attribute of the road ahead. Both need one more thing: traffic volume.

What our data cannot tell you yet

Risk per vehicle (no traffic counts exist for Assam) · exact crash times (police round them) · fog from police forms · the outcome of a single crash (AUC 0.57).
